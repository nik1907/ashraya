How much time do you have with the Ambassador?

Be honest — this determines the entire structure of the presentation.

A

Under 5 minutes

A brief introduction at a community event, a handshake moment, or a corridor encounter. You get one shot — one thing they remember.

Need a single knockout moment — no demo, just the story + show the app exists
B

10 – 15 minutes

A dedicated slot at a formal meeting or community gathering. Enough for one complete story arc — problem, live demo, impact.

The sweet spot — full live demo is very doable
C

20 – 30 minutes

A formal presentation slot. Room for the story, a live demo, a Q&A, and a clear ask at the end.

Full experience — can include data, multi-role demo, and a direct proposal
