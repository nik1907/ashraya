When the AI agent spots a problem — what happens?

The AI reviews the case after submission. It finds something missing or inconsistent. Where does it go?

A

Straight back to the volunteer

The AI sends the volunteer a specific message: "This case is missing a police report number — without it, the Embassy cannot act. Please add it before we forward." The Embassy never sees incomplete cases. The volunteer fixes and resubmits.

Cleanest for Embassy · Volunteer gets actionable guidance
B

TFA Admin review queue

The AI flags the issue and holds the case in an admin queue. A TFA admin reviews it — they either fix it themselves, send it back to the volunteer, or override and forward anyway. Human in the loop.

More control · Slower · Good for sensitive or suspicious cases
C

Forward to Embassy with AI flag attached

The case still goes to the Embassy immediately — but the AI's assessment travels with it: "AI Review: documents incomplete — passport copy missing. Narrative consistent with case type. Recommend requesting documents before action." Embassy decides.

Fastest · Embassy stays informed · They may find it useful
One constraint to keep in mind: The AI agent cannot see passport numbers or Emirates IDs — those stay encrypted and never leave the platform. It reviews the case narrative, documents checklist, dates, employer details, and consistency of the story. That's still plenty to do a meaningful quality check.
