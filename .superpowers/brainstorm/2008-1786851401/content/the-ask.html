What do you want the Ambassador to do when you leave the room?

Every great presentation ends with one clear ask. What's yours?

A

Official Endorsement

The Ambassador publicly recognises Ashraya as the welfare reporting platform for Indian nationals in UAE — a letter of support, a mention in their address, or a social media post.

Builds credibility with all communities · Low commitment for them
B

Make It the Official Channel

The Embassy agrees that welfare cases reported through Ashraya will be treated as formal submissions — volunteers become an extension of the Embassy's welfare desk.

Highest impact · Changes how the Embassy operates
C

Expand to All Indian Communities in UAE

Ask the Ambassador to introduce Ashraya to all registered Indian community organisations in UAE — not just TFA — so the welfare net covers every state and region.

Biggest reach · Positions TFA as a national model
D

No explicit ask — let it land

This is an awareness and relationship moment. You want them to walk away impressed and remembering TFA. The conversation that follows will create its own opportunity.

Lowest pressure · Right for a first introduction
