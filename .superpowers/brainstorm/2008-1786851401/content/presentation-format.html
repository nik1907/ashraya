How should we present Ashraya?

Four approaches — each very different from a traditional slide deck. Pick the one that excites you most.

🎭

Live Story Demo

No slides at all. You open the real app and walk through a single human story in real time — "Priya, a domestic worker in Dubai, goes missing." The audience watches a live case get submitted, the embassy get notified, and an official click the action button — all in one sitting.

Strengths

  • Most visceral, human impact
  • Shows it actually works
  • No risk of "death by slides"

Needs

  • Good internet on the day
  • Rehearsed demo account
📜

Scroll Narrative Web Page

A purpose-built webpage (like a modern charity annual report) that you scroll through together on a big screen. Each section reveals: the problem, the person, the process, the result. Doubles as a leave-behind URL you send to the embassy afterward.

Strengths

  • Works offline, no demo risk
  • Shareable URL after the meeting
  • Highly designed, memorable

Needs

  • Build time (~2-3 hrs)
  • Real stories / stats needed
🗺️

"War Room" Dashboard Reveal

Open the live admin dashboard on screen. Show real (or seeded demo) statistics: cases by type, emirates, severity. Walk the audience through the data like a mission briefing. Then zoom into one case and show the complete journey. Feels like a command centre.

Strengths

  • Data-driven, credible
  • Impresses government / embassy
  • Minimal prep if data exists

Needs

  • Realistic seeded data
  • Dashboard must look polished
⚡

2-Minute Problem + Live Demo

Open with two stark slides: one shocking stat ("X Indian nationals required emergency welfare assistance in UAE last year"), one map. Then immediately switch to the live app. The rest is all demo. Fast, punchy, no fluff.

Strengths

  • Respects the audience's time
  • Hook lands before demo
  • Easiest to prepare

Needs

  • Compelling opening stat
  • Clean demo flow
