Your 12-Minute Presentation — The Full Design

Live Story Demo format · Death & Repatriation case · Double ask at the close

Act 1 — The Hook (90 seconds)
Opening — No slides. Just words.
"Your Excellency, last year, Indian nationals in the UAE faced hundreds of welfare crises — labour exploitation, missing persons, deaths far from home. Most of those cases were reported on WhatsApp groups. Many were lost. The families back in Telangana, in Andhra, in Kerala — they had no way to know if anyone was helping. Today, in 12 minutes, I want to show you what we built to make sure that never happens again."

No laptop open yet. Eye contact. Let that sit for two seconds. Then open the app.

Act 2 — The Story (5 minutes, live)
Minute 1 — Submit the case

Open Ashraya on screen. "I'm going to play the role of a TFA volunteer right now." Enter: Rajesh Kumar, 34, construction worker. Found unresponsive at a labour camp in Sharjah. Suspected cardiac arrest. Family in Karimnagar has not been informed. Hit submit.

Minute 2 — The email arrives

Pick up your phone (or show a second screen). "This is what just landed in the Embassy's inbox — right now, while we're sitting here." Show the email: case details, severity flag, two action buttons. "The Embassy official doesn't need to log in. They click one button."

Minute 3 — The volunteer knows

Switch back to the app. Show the case status updating. "The volunteer who reported this — they're back in their office in Dubai — they just received this." Show the reporter confirmation email. "For the first time, they know it reached the right people."

Minute 4 — The full picture

Open the admin dashboard briefly. "This is what the TFA welfare team sees — every case, every status, every emirate. Nothing lost. No WhatsApp thread to scroll through at midnight." Pause. Close the laptop.

Act 3 — The Human Truth (60 seconds)
After the demo — put the laptop aside
"Rajesh Kumar is a fictional name. But the situation isn't. Cases like his happen every week. What we've built means the Embassy knows within minutes, not days. The volunteer doesn't feel helpless. And the family in Karimnagar — they get an update. That's what Ashraya does."
Act 4 — The Double Ask (90 seconds)
Primary Ask

"We would like the Embassy of India, Abu Dhabi to formally recognise Ashraya as a welfare reporting channel — so that cases submitted through this platform are treated as official submissions, and our volunteers become an extension of your welfare desk."

Stretch Ask

"And if Your Excellency is willing — we'd love your introduction to the other registered Indian community organisations in UAE, so this platform serves every Indian national here, not just our community."

The Close — One Sentence
"We built Ashraya on our own time, with our own hands, because we believe every Indian abroad deserves to know their community has their back — and so does their Embassy."
