Cases grouped by what the embassy must do next. Each lane has a header, cases sorted critical-first inside. Scroll down through lanes. Most actionable — duty officer sees exactly what stage needs attention.

C — Priority-first flat list

All open cases in one list sorted by risk: critical first, then high, then medium. Status shown as a tag. Simplest to scan — duty officer immediately sees the highest-risk case at the top regardless of which stage it's in.
